I build data and AI products for private capital, end to end.

Private capital is money invested in companies and loans that don't trade on a stock exchange: startup funds, private lenders, infrastructure funds. These investors decide with little public data, so the work is turning documents into data they can trust, then into tools that speed up their decisions.

BDC Mark Tape

BDCs (stock-listed funds that lend to private companies) publish every loan they hold. This tool puts those public reports into one table. Lenders use it to price a new loan and to pick which existing loans to check first. Every number links to its SEC filing.

For: A lender pricing a new loan or reviewing its loans
