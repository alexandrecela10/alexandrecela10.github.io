I build data and AI products for private capital, end to end.

Private capital is money invested in companies and loans that don't trade on a stock exchange: startup funds, private lenders, infrastructure funds. These investors decide with little public data, so the work is turning documents into data they can trust, then into tools that speed up their decisions.

Data foundations

Turning messy financial documents into clean, checked data

BDC Footnote Tape

Give it any BDC's 10-K or 10-Q (annual or quarterly report). Get back one spreadsheet row per loan, with each footnote's meaning attached.

For: An analyst at a private lender

Case study DemoInteractive app

BDC Mark Tape

BDCs (stock-listed funds that lend to private companies) publish every loan they hold. This tool puts those public reports into one table. Lenders use it to price a new loan and to pick which existing loans to check first. Every number links to its SEC filing.

For: A lender pricing a new loan or reviewing its loans

Data and AI products

Tools that help investors decide faster, with the evidence shown

Deck Rank

Founders email pitch decks to a startup fund. Deck Rank ranks them so the best one gets read first. Every score quotes the slide it came from.

For: A junior investor at a startup fund

Case study DemoInteractive app

Alpha Scout

Describe the kind of startup a fund wants to back. Get a scored shortlist from the web, each score backed by a quote and a link.

For: A junior investor at a startup fund

Night’s Watch

Warns a lender when a company it lends to may not have enough usable cash for its next interest payment. Then it ranks which companies the lender's staff should handle first, by money at stake.

For: The team that checks borrowers at a private lender

Case study DemoOriginal prototype + deck

Shield

Finds events in a construction project's letters and reports that could cost the owners money. Shows each contract deadline and drafts the formal notice for a named person to approve.

For: A fund that owns part of a large construction project
